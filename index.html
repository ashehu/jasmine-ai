Inside the AI

What happens between the brief and the shortlist.

Jasmine does not ask, “Who is the biggest creator?” It asks, “Who fits this campaign, and what evidence supports that choice?”

Same creator. Different brief. Different ranking.The model evaluates fit relative to the campaign rather than assigning one universal creator score.
